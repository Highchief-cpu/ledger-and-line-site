Selected work

Case study 01

The practice brand we built to prove this process out before taking on clients.

Basin

Skincare, reduced to three products

UI/UX design Framer Copywriting Marketing strategy

Basin is a fictional minimalist skincare brand — three fragrance-free products, no proprietary blends, built as our first practice project to test the studio's full process from research to launch.

Our role

Design, copy, build

Purpose

Prove the process, start to finish

How we work

Six steps, same order, every time

From first conversation to launch, no surprises in between.

1

Research

We learn the business, the market, the competitors, and who the site needs to convince.

2

Strategy

We set the brand and marketing direction before a single pixel or sentence is written.

3

Copy

Messaging, website copy, headlines, and calls-to-action — written to be read, not skimmed.

4

Design

The visual experience takes shape in Figma, built around the words, not a template.

5

Build

We develop the site in Framer, turning the design into something that works in a browser.

6

Launch

We test, refine, and hand over a site that's ready for real visitors on day one.
